# Licensing scope

The repository's principal thesis-template sources state that they are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 United States license. The root `LICENSE` file records that notice for repository users.

The legacy files under `template/acmtrans/` contain provenance comments tracing them to older ACM/Chicago bibliography-style work, but their headers do not state an explicit license. Their inclusion here should not be interpreted as relicensing them under the Creative Commons notice. Preserve their existing notices and provenance when redistributing or modifying them.
